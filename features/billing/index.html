    Billing & Cost Centers¶

    Bambuddy has always been able to tell you what a print cost. Billing tells you whose budget it came out of — and, when you want it, stops a print that would go over.

    It is aimed at installs where somebody has to be billed: a lab charging research groups, a makerspace with member accounts, a department splitting machine time between projects. It is entirely optional, off by default, and nothing about an existing install changes until you turn it on.

    What it does¶

    • Cost centers — a budget somebody can print against: a team, a course, a customer, a grant. Every user also gets a private one automatically.
    • Budgets — an optional total or monthly limit per cost center. A center with no budget is explicitly unlimited, not blocked.
    • Reservations — a queued print's cost is held against the budget while it waits, so ten queued jobs cannot each pass a check that only the last would fail.
    • A ledger — every charge, deposit, withdrawal and adjustment, on one page, with the cost center it belongs to.
    • Server-side costing — the estimate that budgets are enforced against is computed by Bambuddy, not sent by the browser.
    • Proportional charging — a print that fails half way is charged for the filament it actually used.
    • A charging policy — a charge is the filament by default; you choose whether completed and failed prints also pay their energy and printer wear, and how a failed print's filament is charged.

    Turning it on¶

    Settings → General → Cost Tracking

    Setting Default Range What it does
    Billing enabled Off — Turns the whole feature on. While off, no cost center is required, no budget is checked, and the Finance page is not in the sidebar.
    Printer kill switch Off — Stops prints that start without going through Bambuddy. Separate switch — see Printer kill switch below, and read it before enabling.
    Budget reset day 1 1–31 Day of the month a monthly budget window restarts. Clamped on short months, so 31 means the last day of February.
    Budget reset timezone UTC IANA name The timezone that reset day is measured in, e.g. Europe/Berlin. Teams spread across timezones otherwise disagree about which month a print landed in.

    The Finance page appears when billing does

    The Finance entry sits between Statistics and Settings in the sidebar, and only while billing is enabled. If you have rearranged your sidebar, it appears wherever you put it.

    Cost centers¶

    A cost center is created and staffed on the Finance page by an administrator.

    Private cost centers are created automatically, one per user, and are the reason a single-user install works the moment billing is switched on. They belong to their owner: nobody else can print against them, and they cannot be renamed, deactivated or deleted. An administrator can set their budget; a budget of 0 refuses every print with an estimated cost.

    Shared cost centers are created by an administrator, who then adds members. Each member carries a can print flag, so somebody can be given visibility of a budget without being able to spend it.

    Each center can carry a total budget (a fixed pot) or a monthly budget (refreshed on the reset day above). A center with neither is shown as Unlimited — printing against it is never blocked, and its spending is still recorded.

    A cost center in use cannot be deleted

    Deletion is refused while any transaction or active reservation references the center, because removing it would rewrite the history of everyone who ever printed against it. Deactivate it instead — an inactive center cannot be selected for new prints, and its ledger stays intact.

    Balances and budgets are not the same thing¶

    This is the one thing worth reading twice.

    What it means Does it stop a print?
    Balance What a user has spent, deposited and been adjusted by No
    Cost center budget The limit set on the center a print is billed to Yes

    A balance is a record, not a credit limit. A user sitting at a negative balance can still print; only the budget on the selected cost center can refuse a job. If you want spending to stop somewhere, set a budget there.

    A personal balance counts charges with no cost center, plus charges to the user's own private center. A print billed to a shared center does not move the personal balance — it belongs to the team that paid for it.

    What happens when you print¶

    1. Pick a cost center. With billing on, the print dialog shows an estimated cost and a cost center picker. If you have no active center you are allowed to print against, the dialog says so and the print is refused — ask an administrator for print access on one.
    2. The budget is checked against what the center has already spent in the current window, plus anything reserved for prints that have not run yet. If the job does not fit, it is refused with the amounts in the message.
    3. A reservation is taken when the item is queued, and released if the print never happens.
    4. The charge is written when the print ends, from the archive's measured cost rather than the estimate.

    The estimate and the charge are different numbers

    The estimate is what the file and your printer's rates predict; the charge is what the print actually consumed. They will rarely be identical, and the charge is the one that lands in the ledger. The figure in the dialog is a display hint only — budget enforcement uses Bambuddy's own calculation, so editing it in the browser cannot buy a bigger budget.

    Every print is charged at the spools it prints with. When a print starts, Bambuddy estimates its cost: its plate's planned filament at the spools mapped for it, which is the mapping chosen in the print dialog or stored on the queue item, or the loaded trays whose colours match. The print is charged from that start estimate. A reprint is priced the same way, so printing an archive again on a spool that cost more, or after the spool's price changed, is charged at the new price. A first print of one plate of a multi-plate file is charged from that plate's own figure as well, not from the whole file's cost scaled to the plate's share.

    The charge is the estimate made at the start, not the cost measured at the end. The Print Log shows each print's measured cost, which can differ from what was charged; in Spoolman mode that measured cost may not be priced from the spool at all. A completed print with no measured cost, a first print included, is logged at what it was charged. The archive card keeps showing its first print's cost.

    If a print ends before its start estimate was worked out (it failed within seconds, or Bambuddy restarted while it printed), it is priced when it ends, from the spools in its trays at that moment.

    A print Bambuddy could not price from its spools (no mapping, no spool in the mapped trays, or the file is gone) is charged from the archive's cost. The budget a queued reprint reserves is priced like its charge when the queue item has a spool mapping, and is the archive's cost without one. It is worked out when the job is queued and again when it is sent to the printer. By then the queue has mapped the job's filaments to the printer's slots, so a job queued without a mapping is priced at its spools there. The charge can still differ from the reservation if a spool or its price changes before the print starts. In Spoolman mode the reservation finds a spool the way the charge does: by its RFID tag first, then by its slot assignment in Bambuddy.

    A spool of the built-in inventory priced 0 counts as free. A print only on such spools is charged nothing and reserves nothing, and it is not refused for having no estimated cost. This applies to the built-in inventory only: in Spoolman a price of 0 means the spool has no price, and its filament is charged at the default filament cost.

    A built-in spool with no price at all is not a free one. Its filament is charged at the default filament cost. If that is set to 0, such a spool does not price the print: a print only on spools without a price is charged from the archive's cost, and reserves that, not 0.

    With billing off, prints and queued jobs are not priced for a charge, and queueing a job does not ask Spoolman for prices.

    Aborted prints are charged proportionally. A job stopped part way is billed for the filament it consumed, not for the whole job.

    A job is never charged twice. Each dispatch carries its own billing identity, so a reprint of the same file is a new charge, a restart mid-print does not produce a second one, and a charge that was deleted by an administrator does not come back on the next reprint.

    A print is billed to the user who queued it. A reprint of someone else's archive is charged under the person who sent it, not the archive's owner.

    Deleting a user does not cancel the cost of a print still running. It is charged to its cost center when it ends, including the user's own private center (which stays after them), and shows No user in the ledger, as do prints that never had a user (login turned off). Deleting the user together with their items releases the budget their queued prints still held; a print of theirs that is still running is kept until it ends, without a user, and billed the same way.

    A charge that fails is loud. If a charge cannot be written, Bambuddy reports it in the interface and through any notification provider subscribed to the Billing charge failed event, rather than quietly not billing.

    Print charges: energy, wear and failed prints¶

    By default a charge covers the filament: all of it for a completed print, the share it used for a print that failed or was cancelled. Bambuddy also works out two more costs for every print: its energy, measured by the printer's smart plug, and its printer wear, the print's duration at the printer's wear cost per printing hour. The charging policy decides which of these a print pays.

    Settings → General → Cost Tracking → Print Charges, shown while billing is enabled:

    Setting Default What it does
    Completed prints Energy Cost Off Adds the print's energy cost. Needs a smart plug with energy monitoring on the printer.
    Wear Cost Off Adds the print's wear cost. Needs a wear cost per printing hour on the printer; a printer without one adds nothing.
    Failed and cancelled prints Filament charge Amount used Amount used charges the filament in proportion to what the print consumed. Full print charges it as if the print had completed. Not charged charges no filament.
    Energy Cost Off Adds the energy the print used before it stopped.
    Wear Cost Off Adds the wear for the time the print ran.

    A completed print always pays its filament. With the defaults, charges are exactly as before.

    Energy and wear do not depend on the filament

    Energy Cost and Wear Cost are charged whenever they are switched on for how the print ended, also when its filament costs nothing: a print on free spools still used the printer and the power.

    The charge stays one ledger entry, and its description lists what it is made of:

    Print charge: Benchy (filament 1.20 + energy 0.08 + wear 0.25)
Print charge: Benchy (filament 0.60 + energy 0.08) [failed: 50.0% filament (50.0g/100.0g)]
Print charge: Benchy (filament 1.20; filament charged in full) [failed: full filament]
Print charge: Benchy (energy 0.08 + wear 0.25; filament not charged) [failed: filament not charged]

    The part in square brackets is how a print that did not finish ended and what was charged for its filament. The Finance page shows it as a badge on the entry instead of in the description.

    • Energy and wear are the print's actual values, also for a print that did not finish: what the plug measured and how long the printer ran.
    • A print that pays no filament still pays its energy and wear, if those are switched on for how it ended. That covers a completed print with no filament cost (free spools, an archive with no filament data), a failed print under Not charged, and a failed print Bambuddy has no filament data for. Its description then lists only what was charged: Print charge: Benchy (energy 0.08 + wear 0.25). With nothing left to charge, no entry is written and the print's reservation is released.
    • Energy is read a few seconds after the print ends. A print that pays energy, and whose plug was read at its start, is charged once the plug has been read again, instead of at the moment it ends. Every other print is charged when it ends, as before.
    • If the energy cannot be read (no plug, the plug is unreachable, or its counter was reset during the print), the print is charged without it and the description says so: (filament 1.20 + wear 0.25; energy not available).
    • Energy is charged only from one plug. The energy is the difference between the plug's counter at the start and at the end of the print. If the plug read at the start does not answer at the end and another plug of the printer does, the two readings are from different counters, and the print is charged with energy not available.
    • A print that ended while its printer was offline pays no energy. That is any print whose end Bambuddy did not see: the printer was disconnected, or Bambuddy was not running. Its end is noticed at the reconnect, and the plug's reading then also holds everything since the print really ended (idle draw, other prints). Such a print is charged when it is noticed, with energy not available. Its wear is charged when Bambuddy can still work out when the print ended, for that duration, and is nothing otherwise.
    • The policy that counts is the one in force when the print ends. A charge waiting for its energy is not changed by a setting changed meanwhile.
    • A restart does not lose the charge. A charge that was waiting for its energy when Bambuddy stopped is written at the next start, with the energy if it had been saved, without it otherwise. If that fails, the Billing charge failed alert is sent, once, and the charge is tried again at each following start. Whatever removes the print's log entry or its archive while its charge is waiting writes the charge first: clearing the print log or deleting an entry, deleting or purging the archive, deleting its printer with its archives, deleting its user with their items, and merging archives. A waiting charge whose archive is gone all the same cannot be written: the Billing charge failed alert is sent and the print's reservation is released.
    • Budgets cover the filament only. The budget check and the reservation taken for a queued print use the filament estimate, without energy or wear. With those switched on, a center can end up slightly over its budget.
    • The Print Log's cost stays the filament. With Energy Cost or Wear Cost on, the Print Log still shows a print's filament cost as its cost, and its energy and wear in their own fields. The charge is the three together: the filament, priced at the spools the print prints with, plus its energy and wear. So a completed print with no measured cost is logged at the filament part of its charge, not at the whole charge.
    • The policy applies to every cost center.

    The Finance page¶

    Users see their own balance and ledger. Administrators additionally get an Admin view covering every user and cost center:

    • Cost centers — create, rename, deactivate, set total or monthly budgets, add and remove members, and set who may print
    • Transactions — filter the whole ledger by user, cost center and type; edit or delete an entry; move a transaction back to a personal account
    • Deposits and withdrawals — credit or debit a user's account
    • Manual print charge — record a job that happened outside Bambuddy

    Amounts use the currency configured in Settings → General.

    Permissions¶

    Permission Grants
    cost_centers:read_own See your own balance, ledger and the cost centers you may print against
    cost_centers:read_all See every user's balance and the full ledger
    cost_centers:modify Edit cost centers, budgets, members and transactions
    cost_centers:create Create new cost centers

    The default Administrators group holds all four. Operators hold cost_centers:read_own, so they can see their own spending and print against the centers they belong to. Grant the rest to whoever does your accounting.

    API keys¶

    With billing on, a job queued through an API key needs a cost_center_id like any other. A key with Manage Queue finds the ones it may use at GET /api/v1/finance/cost-centers/mine. That endpoint returns the cost centers of the key's owner, and a key can charge only those, or any center if its owner is an administrator. A key created before keys had owners has none, and needs to be recreated to queue with billing on. Balances, the ledger and cost-center administration stay closed to API keys.

    Printer kill switch¶

    Settings → General → Cost Tracking → Printer kill switch. Off by default, and separate from billing itself.

    An install that bills its users can be bypassed by sending a job straight to the printer from Bambu Studio or Handy — that print is not queued, not costed and not billed. With the kill switch on, Bambuddy stops such a print as soon as it sees it, raises an alert in the interface, and sends the configured print-stopped notification. Its archive is marked cancelled, with the reason Stopped by the printer kill switch, so it does not count as a failed print.

    This stops prints, and a stopped print is not recoverable

    Enable it only where prints genuinely must not start outside Bambuddy. It acts on any print it can show did not come from Bambuddy, including one started deliberately by an administrator at the machine. The printer's built-in calibration (started on the printer's screen or from the slicer's device page) is left alone; a calibration print sent from Bambu Studio, such as the pressure-advance line, is a print like any other and is stopped. On an A1, A1 Mini, P1 or A2L the built-in calibration reports itself under a plain file name that any file could be given, so it is stopped too: switch the kill switch off while calibrating those printers.

    It is deliberately cautious about what it can prove. When ownership of a running print cannot be established — during the moments after a Bambuddy restart, or while a job Bambuddy dispatched is still being recorded — it does nothing and logs that it deferred. Stopping a print is irreversible; declining to act costs a log line.

    Limitations¶

    • Balances do not gate printing — only cost center budgets do. See above.
    • Monthly budgets reset, they do not roll over. Unspent budget from last month is not added to this month's.
    • The kill switch works on prints it observes, so it acts after the printer has started rather than preventing the start.

    Troubleshooting¶

    "No active cost center is available for printing" — you are not a member of any active cost center with print rights. An administrator can add you on the Finance page, or check that the center has not been deactivated.

    A print was refused for budget — the message carries the requested amount and what remains. Remember that queued prints hold reservations: cancelling a queued job releases its reservation immediately.

    The Finance page is missing from the sidebar — billing is off, or your account lacks cost_centers:read_own.

    A balance looks wrong after an administrator edited a transaction — balances are recomputed from the ledger, so the ledger is the source of truth. If a figure still looks wrong, an administrator can rebuild the balance ledger from the Finance page.
